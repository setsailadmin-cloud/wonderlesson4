8 Questions • Sequential Order
Question 1 Ch. Choose Kind

What is Mr. Browne’s September Precept, and what does it mean?

Hint/Answer: "WHEN GIVEN THE CHOICE BETWEEN BEING RIGHT OR BEING KIND, CHOOSE KIND." It means that choosing kindness is more important than winning an argument or proving you are right.
Question 2 Ch. Lunch

What does it mean to “save a seat” for someone?

Hint/Answer: Reserving an empty spot next to you at lunch or in class so that a specific friend can sit with you, demonstrating welcoming behavior and friendship.
Question 3 Ch. The Summer Table

Summer tells August that their names “match”. How do they match?

Hint/Answer: Both "August" and "Summer" are connected to the summer season!
Question 4 Ch. One to Ten

August talks about using a “scale of one to ten”. When his mom asks him about his day, he says it was a “five”. What kind of a day did August have?

Hint/Answer: A "five" represents an average or medium day—neither terrible nor amazing, right in the middle.
Question 5 Ch. Padawan

What do you think the term “good call” means?

Hint/Answer: It means a good decision or a wise choice (such as Dad acknowledging Mom's decision to send August to school).
Question 6 Ch. October

How long did it take all the kids in August’s school to get used to his face?

Hint/Answer: It took about one month (by October) for the other students at Beecher Prep to get accustomed to seeing his face.
Question 7 Ch. Apples

What does “cracking up” mean?

Hint/Answer: "Cracking up" means laughing hard or uncontrollably at something funny.
Question 8 Ch. Mr. Browne's October Precept

What do you think “deeds” are, based on what August writes about the precept?

Hint/Answer: "Deeds" are actions or things that people do. Your deeds represent who you are as a person ("Your deeds are your monuments").
